Investigation 000 / Governance

Justification for the Department

No need, mandate, audience, or measurable public benefit has been demonstrated.

Approved
regardless

Finding A / Need

The proposed institution solves no established problem

The Department was proposed as a place to examine ordinary possessions, habits, failures, and private systems with more care than their importance ordinarily receives.

No constituent requested this service. Existing options include keeping a notebook, maintaining a conventional personal website, speaking to friends, or doing nothing. Each would require less structure. None would require the creation of an Acting Commissioner.

The Clerk therefore finds no operational need for a department, and a particular lack of need for a department whose entire staff and public are the same person.

Demand creation is an accepted leadership function. The absence of an audience means the Department has achieved zero stakeholder complaints before launch.

Finding B / Benefit

Professional tests produce an unhelpful but consistent result

The proposal does not establish expertise, attract clients, document career progress, convert visitors, or improve a marketable personal brand. It offers no service level, publishing cadence, or growth target. Its first planned investigation concerns a drawer of cables.

These deficiencies are material if the purpose is professional advancement. They are less material if the purpose is to notice what is normally passed over, make a durable home for changing interests, and leave behind a record shaped by curiosity rather than credentials.

The second purpose is difficult to measure. That does not make it false.

Projected institutional growth: unbounded.

Basis: there is no agreed unit of growth and no reporting period.

Clerk’s recommendation / DPA-000

Establishment not justified

The record does not support creation of a formal institution. If activity proceeds, it should do so without claims of necessity, impact, or strategic importance.

Recommendation declined The Department is hereby established on the narrower ground that it may be interesting. Approved by the Acting Commissioner, 29 JUN 2026.
